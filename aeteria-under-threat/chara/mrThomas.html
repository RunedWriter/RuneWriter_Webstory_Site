Who is Mr Thomas?

Role

Mr Thomas is the human Liaison to Tyr Iffyn of the Void-Born tribes

History

Mr Thomas grew up outside of the sol system. His homeworld was one of the first to be attacked by the symbiants once their 'pilgrimages' were denied and armed incursions began. Having lost several of his family to the war he originally sought to contribute militarily in search of vengeance. After having served 5 years in the conflict however the bloodshed began to feel pointless and he sought ways to contribute to a more permanent and direct avenue of peace. In time after working his way up to a command-staff position he learned of the Void-Born Tribe's request for embassy and put in his application for the Liaison position. After several layers of tests he was approved.

Personality

Mr. Thomas on the clock is often seen as stern but kind. The task comes first but after that you will often find him to be a curious and welcoming person. His still holds darkness in his hard and bitterness towards the alien due to his personal loss and the experiences suffered in the war. However even if he knows he has unfair biases against the whole born of painful experience he tends to judge first and foremost on the individual.

It is Mr Thomas' personal hope that the ambassadorship of the tribes will bear a lasting alliance so that the pressure born of it can lead to an armistice. Few in Aeteria truly believe it possible to push out the symbiant outright given the shear size of their territory, but a lasting armistice born of unwarranted loss to either side could bear at least some time of peace for the living to enjoy. For as he would say, the grudged of the dead lie in the grave.
