Character overview

Lillian Aeter

Principles

Aeterians in general are principled on self actualization. The idea of being self reliant, of meritocracy first and foremost, and of letting their passion and determination create a life and world for themselves. As Regent, Lilian is also principled on the results first, methods second. While she has a preference for benevolent means, nothing will stop her from ensuring the benefit of Aeteria and its people as a whole.

The teachings of the Aeter has mellowed out some of the rigidity of Lillian's work ethic, and in her tenure she has learned to better shoulder the reality of high leadership, that things will fail from time to time, a mistake can and will cost many lives, but that moving forward is the only way to honor them. That leadership is to ensure mistakes like that are minimal more than ensure they never happen. Her principle on leadership is to make sure her administration is about improving the odds of Aeteria, facilitating prosperity, victory, and hope. It is inevitably for the people on the ground to take the actions and examples of leadership into themselves so that the path forward can be paved.

Lilian's principle on family and connections had to take a backseat to her responsibilities to Aeteria, in a sense all of Aeteria is kin to her, and this principle is what has maintained her preference for benevolent means to reach an end, but in the same way families once had to exile some to preserve the whole in times of famine, Lillian will unwaveringly make whatever sacrifices are necessary. However, those she feels she can trust are invaluable to her and give her solace in the small amount of time she has reserved for herself.
