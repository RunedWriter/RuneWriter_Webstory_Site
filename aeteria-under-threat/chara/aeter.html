Who is Aeter?

History

The history of the Aeter is that of a mass ritual gone wrong. The souls of an entire university merged into a single immortal being. After a time of madness, the Aeter's amalgamation of souls and minds became one. Taking the name of the university he went on to seek penance for the harm done in his madness. Eventually, through talent, effort, and simple abundance of time, Aeter grew into a renowned figure and statesman.

While at first his motivation was redemption and absolution. In time Aeter grew passionate about the bettering of others. Eventually rising to become the king of a domain, Aetera's prosperity continued to grow. After centuries of voluntary integration and acquisition after defensive wars Aeteria eventually became the sole nation of humanity. During this time the societal shift into the meritocracy seen in modern Aeteria took form.Having grown from a collection mainly comprised as commoners or low nobility, reinforced by centuries of witnessing the falls and failures of societies reinforced the belief in him that only through constant and devoted effort could a society endure. Dreams and direction could change but only merit could wether the storm of time.

Personality

As a leader and shaper of society, Aeter is known to be bold and straightforward. Humility and subtlety are facets developed through effort and are pointedly reinforced over time to ensure their maintained existence. Aeter despises sycophants and relishes the rare times away from the role of leader when the rare prodigy outdoes him. His greatest failings are like most humans, his tendency to prefer his own wisdom and beliefs over those of others. Like many humans, Aeter too has a handful of vices and indulges that have kept with him through the ages. A fondness for arcane trinkets and crafting. Hand written books. 'Primitive' hunting. And non-stakes poker being a few.

Having started his life in the time of renaissance Aeter tends to hold views regarding diplomacy in a more feudal lens. A supplicant goes to the one of authority, treaties are guaranteed with pragmatic cheques and balances to ensure mutual interest up to and including hostage exchanges. Aeter is one of deep principle, and should that be broken it will not easily be reforged. Terrifying is a quiet and gentle beast roused to wrath.

Voice

I think I am looking for a voice that feels gentle yet powerful. The aeter may be ageless but as time has gone on he has lost the patience for roundabout verbiage. Aeteria is a nation that was built with longevity and the big picture in mind, self reliance is a virtue and allies that cannot be honest with their needs, wants, and deal breakers are simply not worth the time and investment

examples

Aeteria welcomes you, Tyr of ... I asked this of your elders when they requested our acceptance of your embassy. However their answer was incomplete to me. I ask you now. What are the aims of your people in this? Both our people wish to know more of each other and that was merit enough to house you, but Aeteria is not one blessed with time to waste on pleasantries, and at my age I have lost the patience for it regardless. Let us speak freely of our hopes for each other, and then as we come to know the dept of each others principles we can decide if the prize is worth the price.
