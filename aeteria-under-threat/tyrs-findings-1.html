Officially the Aeterian government called themselves an 'Meritocratic Monarchy'. Which gave our translator no end of trouble, only vaguely clarified by the graphical breakdown of its leadership. With their main creeds of 'Confidence is built on hardship.' 'An idea unspoken, is a future left to die.' and finally 'Every choice lays the future's foundation, never let yours be stolen.' the Aeterians organized themselves under the near absolute authority of one called 'The Aeter'. Strangely, as our logs described a 'Monarchy' as a hereditary title, here it is used to reflect the rule of one.

Instead of hereditary heirs, positions of leaderships are challenged at the end of set terms by the holders of the authority just bellow. An official organizer of a residential block challenges one of a district, a district the town, a town the region, then state, then territory then the Aeter himself. The trials serve as a combination of right to rule, and public sport. Where representatives of nearly all trades, professions, and authoritative jurisdictions seek to demonstrate their merit to peers and public alike. 'Only the best should lead, and it's everyone's duty to keep it so.' Was the casual response of one human liaison I'd asked regarding this. It was customary for this branching tournament of merit to be in descending threes. To use the same example, a city district had three official blocks, a city three administrative districts, and up and on until you reached Aeterian territories.

To allow for expansion, each level of organization can expand itself until there is enough qualities to make either an additional expansion above, or to create a new distinction of organization. For example. If a colony was made, you would start with the lowest authority over the population that would fit in a 'city block', and once there were enough, you would split it into three blocks and have a district authority. As there needs to be a position of final authority the maximum for each layer of organization is thus. 1 block. 1 district 8 blocks. 1 city 8 districts, 5 blocks. 1 Region, 8 cities, 5 districts, 5 blocks. The exception of eight is only on the second to highest tier to ensure there is always a single final authority with a council of administration below them.

The highest embodiment of this is the Aeterian Council under the Aeter themself comprised of the territorial leaders of all Aeteria. On paper this made it look as though any citizen in Aeteria had a reasonable shot at the position of Aeter, however in practice the Aeter had been in power at least the several decades of the Human-Symbiant war. Many in the coalition were concerned of a military dictatorship, and it was my job to determine the truth of this matter.
