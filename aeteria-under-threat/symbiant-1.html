alternate take

In the roughly 100,000 light year expanse of the Milky way galaxy there are millions of potentially habitable worlds. While only a fraction became able to actually house life they gave birth to countless varied forms of flora, fauna, wilds and civilizations. The exponential nature of post evolution technology meant by and large the first generation had an unreasonable advantage in the race to the stars. These were called the founding species. One such species became known as the Symbiant.

The Symbiant, cursed and blessed throughout the current galactic core are a species dependent upon others for propagation and survival. Highly intelligent and instinctively focused on the benefit to their species as a whole there was little of the kind of internal strife that delayed so many species' civilizations. They also benefited from being able to make use of the strengths of their host species.

In the early stages of their evolution, they'd take over powerful predators for protection, as well as select herbivores beneficial to cultivation. The improved biological strength, health, and cognitive ability acquired through their varied hosts quickly made the Symbiants the dominant species of their homeworld. Their need for propagation through more host species, as well as the fear of threats unknown naturally drew their eyes outwards into the vastness of space.

Being long lived and generally free of disunity it wasn't long before they crested the final frontier and made contact with the other foremost species of the galaxy. Posing themselves as a federation and promising all the joys of a utopia, the peaceful and more socially enlightened fell into their clutches, not realizing until too late the full cost of bonding with a Symbiant. There were technically no lies spoken, only of omission. Hosts receive all the blessings the Symbiant can manage. This ranges from near perfect health and a naturally enhanced physiology to a more refined control of mana and ones emotions. Over time however, without constant effort to resist the ego of the host grows more and more aligned with the Symbiant, until while two consciousnesses always remain, there is little that goes against the Symbiant's desires.

The sense of betrayal and the resulting disunity led to the collapse of the federation. Those hosting Symbiants became known as the possessed, and the conflict of the un-possessed against the symbiant carved a bloody path of escape as planets were abandoned and populations fled to drift between the stars. These survivors were originally known as the Fallen, and eventually came to call themselves the Coalition.

The 'non-enlightened' and militant of the founding species who had never joined the federation and chose to close themselves off from all outsiders were left alone for an age, but in time the Symbiant deemed the threat of their existence unacceptable and began the second great Symbiant expansion. The Fallen tried to help where they could, but the Symbiant had chosen their targets well. Their surprise attack and possession of the Leviathans turned the tide before the war had truly begun. While the cost of the conflict was great, and all but one last Leviathan was believed killed in the final battle, the last of the founding species were also forced to flee their ancestral homes and seek shelter in the outskirts of space.

And so, of all the first civilizations, it was the Symbiant Union that stood as the sole power in the galaxy. Militant breed species had their numbers controlled and reduced down over the years and, aside from the occasional discovery of a pre or early ftl species to be evacuated by the coalition or integrated by the Symbiant the status quo was maintained. As long as the Coalition maintained limited peaceful interaction or isolation the Symbiant saw fit to leave them alone. All militant actions were reprised against severely but otherwise the effort of attempting to comb the vast empty regions of space for remnants incapable of serious harm was considered a waste of resources.

Then, a small miracle happened. A species that was both enlightened and militant took to the stars undiscovered and unbeknownst to the galaxy at large. Born of the Sol system the species known as Humans didn't use mechanical based communication, but rather applied a form of pseudo technology where arcana and mechanical implement merged around an intended function. The lack of broad frequency communication meant there was no way of noticing their presence without stumbling across them directly. This allowed the newcomers time, time to grow, to settle, to expand into a domain all their own. By the time the Aeterian Dominion was discovered their territory was comprised of Sol, 9 additional core systems, and about a dozen outer systems in varied stages of development.

The Symbiant Union did as was their custom, offering utopia and generous support to the newcomers, even offering to 'uplift' volunteers into the esteemed position of being able to host a Symbiant and enjoy a far longer life, free of disease and fatigue. To be fair to them. This method did usually work. Humanity however already had a substance known as 'elixir' that offered much of the same benefits without taking another being into themselves. Humanity was already prosperous enough for their cultural satisfaction. Lastly and most importantly, the culture of Aeteria, the unified Human nation, was ideologically opposed to living in a Utopia as it was believed unnatural and unfulfilling to the human instinct. Humanity preferred a life of satisfaction over gratification.

For a time the Symbiant were allowed an embassy, however it didn't take long for the Aeter, the leader of Humanity, to uncover the insidious nature of the Symbiant, as well as the mental restrictions subtly placed on the host. The 'ambassadors' were exiled from Aeterian space and Humanity began preparing itself for the potential of war.

With the failure of diplomacy, the Symbiant took some time to decide if the Aeterian Dominion was a threat sufficient to risk Symbiant lives. Deciding to the affirmative they sent the expeditionary fleet. As a brief aside the Symbiant military is comprised in it's vast majority by the 'un-ascended'. Young members of client species not having yet been chosen for privilege of hosting. In this way it is client species that bear the life tax of conflict and the Symbiants only true presence in war is in major positions of Authority to ensure military compliance. Neither the Symbiant or Aeterians had been in a full scale war for some time when the conflict started, given mutual lack of experience and the more advanced technology of the Symbiant a third party would have expected a bloody but guaranteed victory in favor of the Union. Instead, the Symbiant suffered a massacre.

In the later years of the war hindsight gave explanation for such a wild upset. One leading cause is a difference of culture. The Symbiant loathe the loss of life in principle, even the lives of their client species. As such it is Symbiant doctrine to use oversized, heavily shielded and heavily armed battleships to bombard their opponents from extreme ranges. Using integrated FTL jamming fields to disrupt warp travel and force all opposition to advance under fire and fight them under their own terms.

Humanity on the other hand specializes in intuitive and close (near point blank by the standards of space) combat. Their arcane and brutalist nature leaving them to prefer boarding, ambush, and hit and run warfare. The same principle that allowed Humanity to avoid detection became their salvation in the early stages of the war. Humans do not use conventional FTL travel but rather take themselves through an arcane dimension known as 'the void'. This realm was unaffected by Symbiant FTL jamming technology and the Aeterian armada was able to arrive in real space with no warning, with ships accustomed to fighting at great speeds in point blank combat against a fleet designed for the slow and precise weaponry needed to accurately fight at extreme range.

The resulting slaughter and capture of many Symbiant ships became in a roundabout way a detriment for the Aeterians to come. While it had been generations since Humanity had fought a grand conflict the memory and history of brutal wars and the culture needed for victory was still alive within their minds. The galaxy around them however had grown far more pacifistic save for those nurtured selectively to be brutal and seen as barbaric for that nature. This savage and spectacular form of warfare led to division and fear in the coalition. Delaying attempts to reach out hands of aid to the Aeterians for some time.

And so, the first stages of the conflict were in Aeterian favor, but to the Aeterians they now contended with a galaxy that for some time appeared universally intent on their destruction. The cultural consequences of this impression would have lasting impact in the decades to come.
