The Terrors of Space

The Coalition had been a step behind for the entirety of the early conflict. The Symbiant explorers had found Humanity first, by the time the Coalition learned of the discovery the first ambassadors were being exiled and preparations for war had begun. A fleet was prepared to begin assisting in evacuation and as much of the human language that could be intercepted was decoded and learned. However by the time the Coalition fleet was assembled and en route the first battle between Aeteria and the Symbiant expedition fleet had already reached it's brutal conclusion.

The Coalition relief force arrived in Aeterian space to find a graveyard of Symbiant vessels, data pulled from warp probes let them witness the conflict. From the ramming, point blank bombing and boarding to the capturing of the Symbiant flagships. The Coalition combed the wreckage and found salvageable video logs, which allowed them witness to the unforgiving aggression of the human strike teams. Non-war species seemed to shatter on impact. Arcane and physical weaponry sundered passages with no consideration for the dangers of space's vacuum and many, far too many non-combatants suffered the rupturing death of the void. The videos almost entirely ended with silent corridors filled with the sundered bodies of Symbiant and Human alike. The last captured images being the destruction of the recording devices by armored, black-helmed Humans.

There was little doubt that the surviving forces of the Symbiants would take this loss and seek to spin Humanity into monsters in the eyes of the Galaxy. The most important question for the coalition was if this was actually the truth. It was true that the way humanity fought was nightmarish. It was true that the relief force had fled Aeterian space nearly immediately to prevent being seen as hostile and attacked as the Symbiant had been. But it was also true that the Aeterians, the Humans had pushed back the Symbiant fleet, a feat none had accomplished for a millennia. That alone made them worth consideration as a member or ally.

In the end, as Symbiant propaganda spread, emphasizing Humanity's lack of care for collateral damage and willingness to conduct 'uncivilized warfare'. The desire to learn and make use of Humanity's strength, as well as the fear of their character led to the Coalition calling a council meeting to decide the matter once and for all.

----------------------------------------

The High Council of Common representatives, or The Council for short is the highest deciding body of the Starborn Coalition. As the number of species grew and grew categories within the Coalition were made to prevent a voting body growing too large to quickly make decisions while still ensuring a sense of general representation. It is in this way that there is a dedicated representative for the naturally peaceful or prey species, the naturally aggressive or predatorial species, a representative of the prosperous, impoverished, industrial, intellectual, economical, explorer, peace, war, and commoner. These eleven figures decide the course of the Starborn and today they meet to decide the Coalition's approach to Aeteria.
