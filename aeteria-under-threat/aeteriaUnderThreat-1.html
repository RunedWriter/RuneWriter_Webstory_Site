In the Dominion of Aeterian space, in the midst of Humanities great expansion, the day came when strangers from beyond the horizon sought an audience. They promised peace and prosperity, a wondrous union where all peoples lived free of struggle and fear. Even in those early days it was clear how little they understood us.

Out of pragmatic curiosity and the simply desire to know our galactic neighbors. Their embassy was allowed and discourse was opened between our two nations. We, a people of magic and individualism. They, a federation of technology and conformity. We were cautious in how much we shared of our ways and culture. Giving only as much as needed to learn of theirs. The greatest exchange by far was when they allowed us tours and simulations of each other's ships. It was the final exchange before their true nature was uncovered.

The conformity of the Symbiant Union was more than simple culture or nature. The symbiotic creatures that form it's upper class manipulate the minds of their hosts, and through the people's figureheads, they warp the beliefs and values of even those un-enthralled. Which brings us to the confrontation I attend now.

"You lied to us."

"We did not, noble Aeter. We promise utopia and it is so. We offer life and joy everlasting for the uplifted and it is so. We promised those we bond to do not lose themselves, and it is so. Relationships always come with compromise. My host is as precious to me as I am to it. A hierarchy between us doesn't make any of our promises untrue."

"Do not hide behind such attempts, Word-weaver Nova. You and your embassy are not so new to us as to be ignorant of our culture. How deeply we value our individualism and the belief of equal potential. I am not intrinsically any higher than the lowest of my people, it is only deeds that differentiate any of us. Inviting an hierarchy within our own minds is something you know we'd find sickening. It is an enslavement of ones true being."

Nova's expression became exasperated. Or so the translator devise claimed.

"Noble Aeter, you are old enough to be considered a young elder even by our standards. You cannot tell me you do not understand that to keep peace, to keep prosperity there must be sacrifice. You cannot have a society that is open and free that is also peaceful and orderly. Individuals, are individuals. No needs are the same no wants are the same no dreams are the same. For a union of people, even in a single race to live in harmony there must be a relinquishing of the individual to the collective. The common must outweigh the one. To pretend that a mob, unguided, untethered by a commonality can work together and build peace is insanity."

"You seem to underestimate how deeply I, and by extension my people value that problematic individualism. I cannot imagine a life where I am denied the chance to live my own way as 'living'. The nation I helped build isn't one where people stay quiet or compliant but one where all have the right to make things as they'd choose, be it by leaving Aeteria or by proving to their peers their way is best.I have no need of harmony, my nation EXISTS on cooperative rivalry. Forcing order on a species as animalistic as we are is foolish, so all I seek is a nation that holds it's own accountable for their deeds. What you call civilization I call the neutering of minds. There is much, more than most could fathom that I would sacrifice for peace, but not the soul of my people, nor their minds or nature, not one. Take your promise of gilded cages and be gone from my lands!

Do not be foolhardy, Noble Aeter. You know we are the dominant power of the stars! It is our kind nature that even permits this discussion, instead of simply enforcing compliance through military means. You have tamed a few dozen systems. The Union has cultivated hundreds. We want peaceful integration as war is a tedious and wasteful practice. You want a peaceful integration with us, because to fight us would be your end. Our circumstances are not the same. Please do not misinterpret our kindness and patience as weakness.

It was true that the consequences of defying the Symbiant would be more dire than any decision in the history of mankind. The flat reality of it gave enough pause to allow me to rein in my fury. For the first time in centuries, I felt a decision too great for my voice alone. With a deep breath, I continued.

"The only choices you are willing to consider are compliance or conflict?"

"I am afraid so noble one. Your kind appears innovative and adaptive beyond standard. I cannot fathom a circumstance where we'd be content to leave you alone without putting in enough effort to change you that simple integration would be far easier. We either uplift you now, or return you to the dirt.Those are the only ways this story can end."

"Then I'll offer a pause in our negotiations. I'll contain my desire to expunge you from my lands and you'll stave off the drums of war until I can allow the voice of my people to be heard. The ultimatum you force upon us is existential, they deserve the right to express themselves."

"I would like to say take all the time you need, however that would be dishonest. I can spare... two of your months to account for the time it must take to communicate across your dominion. Then I feel bound by duty to demand an answer. May you choose peace, truly we are not as bad as you paint us to be Aeter. Isn't a life of comfort and joy worth some compromise?"

"You will have your answer in two months."
