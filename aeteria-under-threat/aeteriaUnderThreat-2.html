The Decision

Two days after ceasing communication with the Symbiant every station, core world and colony of Aeteria had been made ready for my address. They symbiant knew we had ftl communication capabilities, but we'd yet to let on just how fast we were capable of exchanging information. Their lack of understanding when it came to technomancy being one of the few advantages we had. As I mused, a ding rung out from the table.

"Coffee's ready M'lord. Tho why you drink this when I know you're incapable of physical fatigue I'll never know."

"It is a mental comfort to me Lilian, and a common habit of those who made me. Few of them ever had enough sleep after all. The relief it gave them is something I am still able to experience with every sip. Thank you for not adding sugar to mine, I know you prefer yours sweet."

"Drinking bitter sludge by choice is not a pass time of mine no. Now, M'lord, I do not know what you could want of me here. I feel as repulsed as you do at the idea of having some thing mucking up my mind and changing how I see things. If that's what it takes to have a permanently peaceful and orderly society, I'll take the headaches of managing the idiocies of a human mind any day."

"You really should give black coffee another chance sometime. A childhood experience is an entirely different... sorry, sorry, I'll let it lie. I am aware our views align on the Symbiant ultimatum. What I fear, what I seek your mind on is how to face the eventuality of our resistance. I cannot allow myself to sacrifice who I am, who I feel we are simply for a better life. I'd rather die. What I truly fear is how much we might change. Death or defeat may not be the worst fait that awaits us should war come to pass.

"I'm not sure I follow, Aeter. What could be worse for us than extinction or enslavement?"

"I'm not sure anything would be worse than extinction but that is not the Symbiant's goal. What would to me be worse than enslavement however is losing who we are in our freedom. We have never had a truly existential threat in our history. Nothing can be completely off the table if it means we live to see another day. Even if it means becoming monsters.

Before Lilian could continue I put up a hand.

"Before you speak, I wish to impart a little more. I, am old. Poignant statement it know but we have enjoyed a good life. Aeteria has been prosperous and peaceful for generations now. There are kept mind shards that let people of today taste the suffering of the past, but it is not the same as living it. I know how it feels to have someone's life bleed hot across my hands as they fade to the abyss. I know the savagery people will stoop to when their back is against the wall, their bellies empty, the families dead or worse, desperate. I still dream of it from time to time. Centuries later I still dream of it.

My hands shook slightly. Not enough for anyone to notice, but Lilian was not 'anyone'.

"If the Symbiant are half as powerful as they claim to be we will have to go to places darker than even I can remember. Even if we win, the Aeteria that emerges may be an abomination to the us we are now. I intend to make this clear in my address. I am not asking my people to simply fight or live or die for me. I am asking you all to face losing yourselves in the horrors to come so we can keep the individualism we so cherish."
