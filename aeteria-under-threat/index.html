    The Symbiant Union

    For centuries the galaxy has experienced prosperity, peace, and unity under the uncontested authority of the Symbiant Commune. Scattered tribes of anarchists and dissidents hide, pirate, and homestead out in the dark, inhospitable voids of space. Peace, prosperity, galactic wide development of systems. Utopia eternal... until them, until the damnable humans were found. Aeteria, a dictatorship of oppression and militant expansion. A people of unrelenting will and boundless cruelty. The Symbiant will not rest until the human children, and their species at large is saved the indoctrination of their culture. Peace will reign in the galaxy, even if we must use force to do so.

    The Aeterian Dominion

    A history of bloodshed, a planet scarred by mistakes of war and industry. Divisions thought to be eternal. The Aeter overcame it all. Through elixir our bodies are driven to heights beyond our limits. Through challenge and trial we find satisfaction and confidence in our dreams. Through our will we shape reality, turning creation itself to our task. The Abyss of space stared down at our speck suspended in a sunbeam. We strove out and turned it's challenge into our passion. We trust in those above us not for their name or station, but for their proof of talent and skill. None more so than the Aeter who stands above all. Humanity shall stand before the indifference of the universe and forge ourselves anew until the heat death ends all.

    The Void-Born Tribes

    A pantheon of kingdoms and dynasties laid to ruin. A sea of survivors turned nomads and refugees in the cold expanse of space where life is hard enough to not merit disturbance of greater powers. The Symbiant collective, a history of souls enslaved without ever knowing it. A dream of resistance devoid of hope. I had thought the desire to stand against them dead in me, until rumors of a newcomer began to circulate the tribes. Aeteria, 'Humanity' as they call themselves. I had thought to soon see their like among us, dead eyed survivors of a war they'd never sought. That was decades ago. They are a speck, a handful of systems standing against a galaxy. Yet they stand unbroken and unafraid. Heroes or Madmen, it has yet to be made clear, but the time has come to know. For the first time in memory, my heart stirs with the embers of hope.
