The Aeterian Project

The stories and setting of Aeteria under threat and the Aeterian universe is one of ideology and grim reality. In Aeteria under threat you will experience the tale of false dystopia and false utopia from the party of the adamantly free and unrestricted coalition. The question of this tale is what is worth sacrificing your freedom for? Peace and prosperity? Individualism and satisfaction? Community and survival? Perhaps all, perhaps none. Tales of the ambassador is a resurrection of a personal project, one that had a good premise, but not enough soul to give it life. Now, in a universe of science, magic, and passion it tells the story of Humanity's entry into a wondrous universe, a terrifying universe, a beautiful universe, and a hateful universe.

There will be eventual expansions to this story, and it is very likely that the conflict found here will prove but an introduction to a larger narrative realm. For now however I seek to breathe the life and soul of genuine conflict and character into a premise left behind and rediscovered. As you read through the comedy, the pain, the struggles and joys of these characters, I only hope I do my job well such that by the time this series ends, they will feel as alive as you or I.

Fare thee well, beloved reader.

You're ever grateful author aspirant.
