The Note

If the god of fatigue had an avatar, I must have ben it.

Two weeks now, two weeks of feeling like I hadn’t slept a wink. Two weeks of nothing being where I left it. Of misplaced items, missing food, and furniture never being where I left it when I went to sleep. That was the one common factor. It was only after I had slept that things were wrong.

I had no stock in my home being haunted, not a single ward showed signs of damage. If not a ghost, perhaps a homeless child I thought. However if it was… they’d have put a cutpurse to shame. No tracks, no shifted dust, no signs of forced entry or scratches on locks. Not a single clue.

Running out of ideas, and feeling tired enough to justify a little madness. I put quill to parchment, and wrote out a letter to leave by my bedstand. Everything was tied to me going to sleep after all, perhaps they’d see it, see it and… write a reply… What could I say? I was grasping at straws and had long run out of sheep to count.

“Dear
Wretched
To the haunter of my home and bane of my rest

If you do exist, and are not just an unprompted fit of Somnambulism (sleep walking) I implore you for a reply. If not to account for the constant disruption of my days as I find my effects misplaced, then for the food and quarter you have allowed yourself without permission.

I believe, as my exhaustion and the disarray of my home coincide near perfectly, that you are to blame for my restless nights and present state, where even to focus on the needs of the day is a war waged in every step. Can you not find succor at the church? Are their alms not enough to stave your hunger that you must take from my larder and disrupt my sleep?

I laugh at myself, knowing it is unlikely you can read these runes, but if you can I truly do demand a response. It is but the least you could do.

With Regards

Richard Quill"

This is gettin' absolutely ridiculous now!

For the past two weeks, I’ve been stuck wakin’ up with too much energy, and not enough to do! Sure, it’s great for my job, but the feelin’ of restlessness is never endin’. If that weren’t bad enough, some jackass of a ghost or somethin’ keeps bargin’ into my home and movin’ it all about!

It’d be fine if they were just hauntin’ me, but they actively cause so much trouble! They use my cleanin’ stuff, my utensils, even movin’ around the chairs like they own the place! Just as I put it down to spirits an’ the like, they leave a note like they own the place! Flowery writin’ an’ all!

Whoever keeps messin’ with me looks to be richer than a pair of gold cufflinks… I should make those tomorrow. Right now, before the dizzy feelin’ strikes again, I should be polite to write back to this potential posho ghost.

"To whom it
Hello there
Listen here, sir and/or madame ghost

Not only do I exist, but I can barely sound out Sonmab ... Somam that word about walking and sleeping. As for your bold asumption of this being your home, kindly bugger off with that as it’s my home that you’ve been moving around without any care in the world!

Becoz of your antics, all my work has been delegatedd moved to the night for not only my work, but to make sure my home gets back to normal. Maybe instead of blaming an inocent person, you should have your head checked for rot or something else. Maybe that’ll cure you of your entittlmente. Hell, maybe take your own advice to go to Church - I hear they’re kind to loonies.

I’m hoping this note has at least gotten through to you and to not touch my stuff again. If it hasn’t… then at the very least get your own milk in! You use too much of it!

Signed,
The owner of this house!
Hazel B."

----------

Well, at least I have a name to work with now. Hazel B., I’d have to stop by the town hall to inquire if there is any record of them.

Hazel's literary... acumen inclined me to break out my finer roasts of coffee. My annoyance at their poor grammar overshadowed by my relief at the vagabond's willingness to respond. Why and how could they be so delusional as to believe this was their home though? Resisting the foolish urge to bring out the deed and leave it as proof (my bad habit of wanting to prove the truth of my words regardless of the wisdom or risk needling me inconveniently), I instead thought on how to resolve this matter and remove this Hazel from my life.

Up until now I had wanted to avoid involving law enforcement, or the healing mages of the clergy. On the former, the punishment for theft, breaking and entering, frankly for far too many things, is a thorough beating at the lightest, climbing with little cause to life indenturement. As for the latter, while Somnambulism itself is little issue, if it was deemed that I was at risk of possession or was tainted by a genuine haunting, I would be the one at great risk.

There was of course the option of calling in a trusted friend in the clergy to catch the intruder unawares. However that would require calling in a significant favor, and one hard bought at the time. For such minor things as a little food and a house in disarray I hadn't wanted to do so yet. Should things continue like this however. Perhaps in as little as a few days, I may be forced to do so.

What could I say to convince this stranger to leave? For now, lets at least try a ... diplomatic approach.

"To Hazel B.

I can assure you that the deed of this house, 482 Baker St, has Mr. Richard Quill written in plain ink. If you doubt it you are free to inquire at the town hall yourself! It is open day and night to accommodate the town's varied needs if you weren't aware. Also PLEASE stop using the good coffee! I had some today and it was down by HALF! That is something I keep for special occasions and is nearly ten times the cost of the coffee found three cupboards down.

Listen, I would prefer there to be a way for this to be resolved without relying on law enforcement. The city's laws are cruel and unjust by my reckoning. If it is a matter of work, or connections I can reach out to assist your finding accommodation and income. I even have a friend in the clergy that can help you if you suffer any physical or mental ailment that is the driving force behind your deluded claim. However this is my home, and I cannot simply tolerate it's intrusion indefinitely. If we cannot come to some accord for you to leave, I will be forced to find a way to make you leave.

May the fates be kind

Richard Quill"

THE ABSOLUTE CHEEK OF THIS GUY!!

The fact he still thinks he’s the owner of my house is insultin’! The first time, I just assume it’s ‘em bein’ negligent, or a bit confused, but to double down on such a thing makes his frilly words feel silly. After finishin’ up a piece of furniture for the lads at the Ruined Arms pub, I actually wen’ to the town hall jus’ to even check my own deed. Silly as it might seem, I ain’t takin’ no chances with whatever this entitled pin’ead… that an’ I ‘ad to ‘ave one of the more ready folks ‘elp me with the letter.

“He’s saying you have no claim to your home and to seek help, or move out,” the nice lady said. I was so angry at that prick’s entitlement, I asked to see my ‘ouse deed. Sure enough, clear as frickin’ day, my name, on my home, at my address. I asked for an extra copy, which she gave me (for free too, an’ a little giggly when askin’ about my work studio… no clue what for). She also said whoever wrote the note is “probably well-educated and wanting to swindle money out of hard-working peoples,” whatever those are. She said nothin’ about the name though, so I guess it wasn’t too important.

As much as ‘is (I assume it’s a he by the name) writing is tidy an’ all that, the bloke really is barkin’ up the wrong tree.
