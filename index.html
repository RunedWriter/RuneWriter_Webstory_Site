About The Author

Runed here. What to say about myself... it's a bit difficult to be honest. Guess I'll start with the basics. Identify as he/him, come from the states, am a nerd and love to write. Writing has been my one consistent productive hobby since I was a kid and now, after many years of practice I feel tentatively ready to put my work out for the world to see.

My writing style is mainly focused on characters, their inner thoughts mixed with observations, and reading flow. The stories I most love writing about are fantasy, sci-fi, and sci-fi fantasy within a universe designed to make the magic believable to some extent. My favorite universes so far are Halo, Warhammer and WH 40k, Frieren beyond Journey's End, HunterXHunter, and Overlord.

I suppose my goal for writing is to create worlds for readers to take a break in, a place for you to escape reality, laugh, dream, fantasize and relish. It is also a passion of mine to write stories where there are views and cultures different from the norm. Now, enough about the man behind the curtain. Please enjoy Rune's Library!
